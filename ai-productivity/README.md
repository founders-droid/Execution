# AI productivity
Prompts, workflows, and experiments for using AI to speed up product execution.

## What goes here
- `prompts/` — reusable prompts that worked well
- `workflows/` — step-by-step AI-assisted workflows (e.g., research → PRD)
- `experiments/` — what we tried, the result, and whether to keep it
